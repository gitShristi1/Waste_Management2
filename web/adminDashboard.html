About EcoCycle

EcoCycle is a waste management platform designed to make the process of recycling simple, organized and efficient. The platform connects users and recycling companies through a single digital system.

Users can sell their recyclable waste, purchase recycled products and interact with recycling companies. Companies can manage waste collection, sell recycled products and manage their transactions through the platform.

The admin manages and monitors the overall EcoCycle system. The admin can view user activities, company activities, transactions, products and feedback to ensure that the platform operates smoothly.
